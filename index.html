What is Direct Primary Care (DPC)? +
Direct Primary Care is a membership-based model where you pay a low monthly fee directly to the practice — no insurance billing, no co-pays, no surprise charges. You get longer appointments, direct access to Dr. Hector, and transparent pricing. It's medicine the way it was meant to be.
Do I need insurance to be a patient? +
No. As a Direct Primary Care practice, we do not bill insurance. Your monthly membership covers your primary care visits. We recommend pairing your DPC membership with a catastrophic or high-deductible health plan for specialist care and hospitalizations.
How quickly can I get an appointment? +
Same-week appointments are typically available. For urgent sick visits, we do our best to see you as quickly as possible. Call us at (317) 888-2737 or submit the contact form on this page.
What conditions does Dr. Hector treat? +
Dr. Hector provides comprehensive family medicine including annual physicals, preventive screenings, chronic condition management (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and more), medication reviews, same-week sick visits, and family health care for patients of all ages.
Do you see Spanish-speaking patients? +
Yes. Hector Family Medicine welcomes Spanish-speaking patients. You can also explore this website in Spanish using the language toggle in the navigation bar above.
